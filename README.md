# FairChain BD – AI-Powered Supply Chain Monitoring Platform

**Live Demo:** [https://fairchain-frontend.vercel.app/](https://fairchain-frontend.vercel.app/)

FairChain BD is an AI-powered supply chain monitoring platform designed to improve transparency, accountability, and price monitoring across Bangladesh's essential commodity supply chain.

The platform tracks essential commodities such as rice, soybean oil, onion, sugar, and lentils throughout different stages of the supply chain — from importers and manufacturers to distributors, wholesalers, and retailers.

FairChain BD aims to help regulators identify unusual price fluctuations, detect suspicious market activities, monitor commodity movements, and make data-driven decisions to prevent market manipulation and unfair pricing.

By integrating machine learning-based price forecasting and anomaly detection, along with interactive geographical visualization, the platform provides valuable insights into market trends and supply chain activities.

### Key Features

- **Supply Chain Tracking:** Monitor commodity movement across importers, manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers, and retailers.
- **Price Monitoring:** Track commodity prices at different stages of the supply chain.
- **AI-Based Price Forecasting:** Predict future commodity prices using Facebook Prophet.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Identify unusual price and supply patterns using Isolation Forest.
- **Interactive Map Visualization:** Visualize supply chain activities and commodity distribution across Bangladesh.
- **Inventory Management:** Monitor product quantities and inventory across different supply chain stages.
- **Shipment & Transfer Management:** Track import shipments, production batches, and product transfers.
- **Role-Based Access:** Provide dedicated access for different supply chain participants and administrators.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Provide an overview of supply chain activities, market trends, and potential irregularities.

### Technology Stack

**Frontend:** React.js, Tailwind CSS

**Backend:** Python, Django, Django REST Framework

**Database:** PostgreSQL

**Machine Learning:** Facebook Prophet, Scikit-learn (Isolation Forest)

**Map & Visualization:** Leaflet, OpenStreetMap

**Deployment:** Vercel (Frontend), Render (Backend)

### Project Objective

The primary objective of FairChain BD is to build a transparent and data-driven supply chain monitoring system that supports fair pricing, reduces information gaps, and helps authorities monitor the distribution and pricing of essential commodities across Bangladesh.
